# Comet Waitlist - spacefs.com Alignment & Enhancement

## Executive Summary

The Comet waitlist website has been completely re-aligned with **spacefs.com's positioning and messaging** while maintaining Comet's unique differentiators (mobile-first design + phone uploads). The site now emphasizes:

✅ **Instant access** - Stream files in real-time, no waiting  
✅ **Zero disk space** - Terabytes accessible without local storage  
✅ **Real-time collaboration** - Teams sync instantly across devices  
✅ **Mobile uploads** - Capture and share directly from phone (Space differentiator)  
✅ **Works with apps you use** - DaVinci, Premiere, Photoshop, etc.

## FAQ Section Alignment

The FAQ should address Space-like concerns:

1. **What is Comet?**
   - Cloud streaming service for terabytes of files
   - Works across phone, desktop, laptop, web
   - Real-time sync and collaboration

2. **How is Comet different from Space?**
   - Mobile-first design (Space is desktop-focused)
   - Phone upload capabilities (shooting directly from device)
   - Designed for both individuals and teams from day one

3. **How does Comet save disk space?**
   - Streams files on-demand (zero local storage)
   - Caches only what you touch
   - Files stay in cloud, visible in your library

4. **Works with my apps?**
   - DaVinci, Premiere Pro, Photoshop, Figma, etc.
   - Standard filesystem access
   - No plugins or extensions needed

5. **How do files stay in sync?**

   - Changes appear in seconds

   - iOS (iPhone/iPad)
   - Android
   - macOS
   - Windows
   - Web
