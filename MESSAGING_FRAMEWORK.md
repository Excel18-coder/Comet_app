# Comet Waitlist - Messaging Framework

## Core Message Hierarchy

### Primary Message (Hero)
**"Your files. Everywhere. Without filling your device."**

Supporting: "Cloud storage designed to make your files feel like an extension of your phone and computer—not a separate place. Access more of your library without keeping all of it stored locally."

### Secondary Messages (By Section)

#### The Simple Idea
- **Device:** 128 GB physical storage
- **Cloud:** 2 TB unlimited storage
- **Access:** Your full library, anytime

#### Without vs With Comet
| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| Phone constantly full | Access your full library anytime |
| Manual file management | Smart storage management |
| Delete files you want to keep | Keep files safely in the cloud |
| Upgrade devices for more space | Free up space without losing files |

#### The Problem
"Your device shouldn't limit how much of your digital life you can access."

#### How It Works
1. **Store** - Keep files safely in the cloud, so your full library can be bigger than your device.
2. **Access** - Browse and open files from phone or desktop whenever you need them.
3. **Free up space** - Keep only what you need locally while everything else remains safely available.

#### Who It's For
- **Everyday users** - Your phone is always almost full, but your photos and videos still matter.
- **Students & professionals** - School and work files follow you between devices.
- **Creators** - Large media libraries stay available without upgrading devices.

#### Free Up Your Device
- See what is taking the most space
- Tap "Free up space" for selected files
- Open cloud files later when you need them

#### Your Storage Follows You
- One library. Multiple devices.
- Same account across phone, desktop, laptop, and web.

#### What Matters Most
**"Not just backup. Everyday access."**

1. **Always in your library** - Cloud files stay visible even when local copies are removed.
2. **Find → Open → Use** - Open what you need without manual browser-download-folder steps.
3. **Built for mobile + desktop** - Designed from day one for phones and computers, with web access too.
4. **Offline choices** - Choose files you always want available offline on your current device.

#### Join the Waitlist
"Your storage shouldn't stop at your hardware."

Supporting: "Comet is in development. Join now to receive early-access updates as we roll out the first private beta."

## Talking Points for Marketing

1. **The Problem We Solve**
   - People run out of device storage constantly
   - They delete files they want to keep
   - They buy bigger, more expensive devices
   - They manually manage files between devices
   - Traditional cloud storage feels separate

2. **Our Solution**
   - Access your full library without keeping everything locally
   - Smart storage that knows what you use most often
   - Free up space without losing files
   - Same library across all your devices
   - Simple, transparent experience

3. **Core Benefits**
   - **More usable storage** - Your device isn't limited by physics anymore
   - **Files stay accessible** - Cloud files are always visible in your library
   - **Free up space anytime** - Remove local copies, keep cloud copies safe
   - **Your library follows you** - Same files on phone, desktop, laptop, web
   - **Intelligent balance** - Frequent files local, rarely-used files in cloud

4. **For Each Audience**
   - **Everyday users** - Never delete photos you care about again
   - **Students** - All your school files accessible from any device
   - **Professionals** - Work files follow you across devices
   - **Creators** - Store massive media libraries without upgrading hardware
   - **Developers** - Access project files and datasets from anywhere

5. **Common Questions**
   - **Is this just backup?** - No, it's about access. Backup is part of the story, but the bigger idea is daily use.
   - **Will it replace local storage?** - No, local storage still matters. This makes it smarter.
   - **When is it available?** - We're currently in waitlist phase. Early beta access coming soon.
   - **Which platforms?** - Long-term: Android, iPhone, Windows, macOS, Linux, Web.
   - **How much does it cost?** - Pricing details coming as we approach launch.
